# Payment logo sources

These marks identify payment providers; they are trademarks of their respective owners. Their presence in the UI does not guarantee availability for a particular ekPay transaction.

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express: [Active Merchant Payment Icons](https://github.com/activemerchant/payment_icons) (MIT-licensed icon set).
- bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, TAP: [Bangladesh MFS Logos](https://github.com/helloanaroul/bd-mfs-logos). The repository states that the marks remain the property of their owners.
- TakaPay, Bank Asia, AB Bank, Dutch-Bangla Bank: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/).
- BRAC Bank, Eastern Bank, City Bank, UCB: [Wikipedia logo files](https://en.wikipedia.org/).
